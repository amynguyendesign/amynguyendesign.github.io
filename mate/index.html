A way of seeing

Check. Then check again.

Checkmate means the king is in check and there is no legal reply. A cramped king alone isn’t enough.

  1. Look at the king’s box.The squares around the king: covered, occupied, or open. “King’s eye view” shows which is which. Tap a marked square to see why.
  2. Try a forcing move.Tap your piece, then a highlighted destination. In mate in 2, find a first move that forces mate against every reply. The trainer plays one defense for you.
  3. Run the three-part check.Can the king escape? Can a defender capture the checker? Can a piece block the check? The trainer checks all legal replies, not just king moves.

King captures are counted under Escape. Knights, pawns, and adjacent checks cannot be blocked. If a king isn’t in check but has no legal moves, that’s stalemate, not mate.

Hints help you learn; unassisted solves fill the five practice marks. “Mate or not?” trains verification rather than moving pieces.
